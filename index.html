OUR PRINCIPLES

Useful AI should be clear about what it changes.

Original text and AI notes stay distinct.

Our design goal is to keep source content visible alongside separately labeled summaries and explanations, so users can review extraction errors.

Privacy is part of the build.

Before handling real documents at scale, NEVTEK needs clear retention rules, secure processing, and an accurate privacy policy.
